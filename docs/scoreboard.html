Live scoreboard — every game, plus its scoring watch

Reads the official MLB schedule and game feeds in your browser and shows every game on the selected date with live score, inning, count, runners and hits/errors — next to the scoring story this repo tracks: exact official-scorer pending markers, scoring changes observed between two reads of the feed, reviews carried in the payload, and the committed model's batted-ball distribution (hit / out / fielder's choice / error / sacrifice…). Polling is best-effort, not real time. Works for any date; today's slate is whatever the official schedule returns.

What the badges mean — and what they do not. ⚖️ Official-scorer pending appears only for the exact MLB StatsAPI registry values os_ruling_pending_primary / os_ruling_pending_prior or the exact description “Official Scorer Ruling Pending”. A play with no result.eventType but no such marker is shown as no event type in this capture and is not reported as a pending decision. ✏️ Scoring changes are differences between two reads of the same play by this browser's ledger — the feed publishes no change marker, so an initial call and a final ruling are known only from consecutive observations. Model numbers estimate the final captured feed label from batted-ball physics and pre-pitch state; they are review aids, never rulings, and the official feed remains the record.

Games

Cards are one per game for the selected date. “Batted balls” counts plays the shared scorer returned with a final captured class; “model predictions” counts rows with a complete Statcast vector. The top-error-risk play is ranked by the binary model's estimated probability that the captured feed label is error (SCORE/100) with its percentile among the fitted batted balls — a ranking, not a forecast of a scoring change.

Run it yourself

This page reads only public MLB endpoints (statsapi.mlb.com), the committed model (data/model.json) and this browser's own observation ledger. If an endpoint is unreachable the page keeps what it has and shows the failure — it does not fabricate a game.
