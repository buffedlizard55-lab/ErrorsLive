Known limitations (don't read past these)

  • 24 labeled errors is a thin positive class. AUC CI [0.52, 0.75] overlaps coin-flip; treat /100 scores as directional, not precise. Growing the sample is fix #1.
  • Error label is a lower bound: throwing-error runs hidden inside 'single' etc. aren't labeled; scorer revisions after game end aren't captured in the feed.
  • No fielder positioning / hang-time features: two grounders at 98 mph are not the same play when one is at a shifted shortstop. Requires defense-tracking feeds (next data tier).
  • Run-removal flag is a heuristic, printed in-page; a true run-removal ledger needs per-play boxscore-diff.
  • Replay video links decayed at source (MLB platform migration); site links regenerate via Film Room search, but some 2014–15 clips may be unrecoverable online.
  • June–2026 pool: season-fatigue and weather effects on error rates are unmeasured.
