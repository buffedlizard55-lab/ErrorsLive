Design decisions that guard the numbers

· The sample is error-ENRICHED and is NOT self-weighting. The 24 games were drawn from a 222-game June–September 2026 pool at three different rates by stratum (A ≥3 team errors 10/20 = 0.500; B = 2 → 10/51 = 0.196; C ≤ 1 → 4/151 = 0.026). Any pool-representative rate therefore requires the raking weights A 2.0 · B 5.1 · C 37.75, which reconstruct the pool's 1.113 team-errors/game as 1.258. The 1.91% error rate quoted on this site is the sample rate, not the pool rate, and the fitted objects model P(class | batted-ball features) within a play, which is far less distorted than the game-level rate. Full table on Methods.
· Calibration checked out-of-fold: 5-fold stratified CV, log-loss & Brier on held-out folds; isotonic recalibration improvement reported separately rather than smuggled into the headline metrics.
· Uncertainty: 1,000× bootstrap over rows for the OOF AUC (95% CI shown) and for the EV coefficient.
· Labels are the official play-result eventType, and nothing is imputed. Errors charged inside other eventTypes (a throwing error on a play scored a single) are not labelled “error”, so the observed error rate is a lower bound — see data/data_quality.json and Methods.
