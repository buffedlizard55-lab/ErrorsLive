Design decisions that guard the numbers

· Self-weighting sample: 24 games drawn with a uniform 0.5 sampling fraction in every error stratum (A ≥3, B =2, C ≤1 team errors) of a 222-game pool — raw averages over this sample are unbiased for pool averages; no post-stratification weights are applied because none are needed.
· Calibration checked out-of-fold: 5-fold stratified CV; log-loss & Brier reported on held-out folds; isotonic recalibration improvement reported (and kept tiny by construction).
· Uncertainty: 1,000× bootstrap over rows for AUC (95% CI shown) and for the EV coefficient.
· Labels are the official play-result eventType. Errors charged inside other eventTypes (throwing errors after a hit) are not labeled “error”, making the error rate a lower bound — see data_quality.json & Methods.
